Mini Game Battle

Two players · one board · five minutes

How to Play

  • You take turns. Roll the dice and move forward that many tiles.
  • The tile you land on tells you which minigame you play.
  • A correct action gives +1 point.
  • A wrong action gives −1 point.
  • Your score never goes below zero.
  • The match lasts 5 minutes. The timer keeps running the whole time.
  • When the timer reaches 0, the player with the higher score wins.
  • After both players finish one lap, the board gets a new minigame layout.
